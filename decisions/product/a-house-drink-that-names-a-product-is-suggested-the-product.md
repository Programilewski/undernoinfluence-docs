# A house drink that names a catalogue product is suggested the product

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** 2026-11-24, `v0.1.21`
**Area:** Catalogue | Sourcing

---

## Problem

"MARTINI VIBRANTE 300 ml", under "DRINKI BEZALKOHOLOWE" at Restauracja Navona, was read as a house drink and approved as one in six seconds, although Martini Vibrante 0% was already in the catalogue. A house-drink line was never compared with the catalogue, so nobody was offered the product. Martini Vibrante is an alcohol-free aperitivo made from dealcoholised wine and usually served with tonic, so a 300 ml serve is almost certainly the product with tonic. The drink in the glass is the product, and UNI missed it.

## Options considered

Leave house drinks as they are, so a mixed serve of a product stays a house drink. Suggest a catalogue product whenever its name appears in a house-drink line, and let a person decide.

## Decision

**A house-drink line whose name contains a catalogue product's name is suggested that product,** and a person confirms it or keeps the house drink. Paweł chose this over leaving it, because otherwise every Martini, Aperol 0.0 or Tanqueray 0.0 served mixed goes unrecorded as the product it is.

## Rules

The product's name, strength words aside, must be printed whole in the line, never as part of a word, and at least four letters long. The menu must give the product's strength (in the name, the heading or the printed column), as for any product. Shared recipes are not suggested this way. The line then waits as "Sugestie", "Popraw" opens on the product, and switching to "Drink lokalu" is one click. A line confirmed as the product is recorded as a product, not as a house drink as well.

## What this prevents

A venue listed as serving "Martini Vibrante" the house drink, which a person looking for the product would never find, and a catalogue product that never collects the venues that pour it.

## Revisit when

Suggestions on house drinks are mostly wrong (a cocktail named after an ingredient that is not the product), and pressing C to keep the house drink becomes the slow part of review.
