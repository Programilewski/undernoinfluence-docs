# A strength printed beside a line vouches for it, and 0,5% counts as alcohol-free in words

**Date:** 2026-11-24
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** 2026-11-24, `v0.1.21`
**Area:** Catalogue | Sourcing

---

## Problem

Taproom's tap list prints each beer's strength in its own column under a style heading: "Mała Czarna", section "IPA 35", strength "0,0%". Creating "Mała Czarna Bezalkoholowe" was refused with "Nie zapisano", as were "PINTA Mini Maxi Jasne Non-Alcoholic" and "Motocyklowe Bezalkoholowe", both printed "0,5%". The guard read only the printed name and the heading, so it concluded the menu said nothing about strength when it said it exactly. Paweł hit the same refusal twice in one batch.

## Options considered

Keep the guard as it was, and have the person strip the strength from the product's name. Let the printed column count only when it repeats the product's exact claim ("0,0%" for a name saying "0.0%"). Let it count, and treat a printed 0,5% or less as satisfying a name that says alcohol-free only in words.

## Decision

**The printed strength counts like a heading: it vouches for this line, and the spelling is not kept as an alias.** And, as Paweł decided, **a printed 0,5% satisfies "Bezalkoholowe", "Non-Alcoholic" or "Free" in a product's name**, because that is the catalogue's own definition of alcohol-free. A name that states "0,0%" asks for more than 0,5% and is still refused.

## Rules

A product's strength claim is accepted from a menu line when the printed name, the section heading or the strength printed beside it gives it. A printed strength above 0,5%, or one with no number, vouches for nothing. Only the printed name can become a confirmed spelling. The menu reader copies the strength exactly as printed or leaves it empty, and each menu is read twice, so the column is trusted as far as a heading is.

## What this prevents

A real alcohol-free beer refused because its menu is a table rather than a sentence, which is how tap lists are printed. And a product being renamed just to get past the guard, which would leave the catalogue less precise than the menu.

## Revisit when

A menu turns out to print the strength column wrongly (a "0,0%" against an alcoholic beer), or a reading invents a strength the menu does not print.
