# No pay-to-rank, no promoted placement, no bought badges

**Date:** 2026-04-22
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** standing rule — nothing to execute once; it binds future work
**Area:** Brand | Analytics

---

## Problem

Promoted placement and paid ranking boosts are easy revenue for a directory platform. A venue pays to appear at the top of search results or earn a premium badge. This is how most local directory businesses monetize. But UNI's core value proposition is data authority — users trust the rankings because they reflect real NoLo commitment, not ad spend. Introducing paid placement directly destroys that trust.

## Options considered

**Promoted placement** — venues pay to appear above organic results. Standard directory revenue model. Immediately raises the question "did this venue rank because it's good or because it paid?"

**Paid badges** — a "featured venue" badge purchasable separately from the earned verified checkmark. Dilutes the meaning of all badges on the platform.

**B2B analytics subscription only** — revenue comes from selling aggregated, anonymized demand data back to venue owners. The rankings and checkmarks stay earned, never bought.

## Decision

No pay-to-rank, no promoted placement, no bought badges, ever. The only revenue mechanism is a B2B subscription that gives venue owners access to analytics they can't get elsewhere — search demand, visibility data, category trends. Rankings are determined solely by credibility score. The verified checkmark can only be earned through data quality.

## Rules

No venue can pay to influence its position in the discovery list, map results, or SEO pages. No "sponsored" or "featured" label may appear adjacent to venue cards in the B2C product. The verified checkmark is not a purchasable product. Premium B2B tiers may unlock additional analytics views but must not affect any B2C ranking or visibility surface.

## What this prevents

Prevents the trust collapse that hits every monetized directory eventually — when users realize rankings are purchased, they stop trusting the platform and it becomes worthless to both sides. Prevents venue owner resentment: if a competitor can buy their way above you, the whole system feels unfair. The analytics-only model means every venue knows exactly what they're paying for, and it has nothing to do with who else ranks.

## Revisit when

Never, for pay-to-rank. This is a core brand and integrity commitment, not a temporary V1 constraint. The analytics subscription model can be extended (more data, more views, more granularity) but the monetization mechanism stays B2B analytics only.
