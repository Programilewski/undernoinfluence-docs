# Ranking is never for sale

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** yes — verified in the code 2026-09-14 (stated on /o-nas)
**Area:** Brand | Venues | Scoring

---

## Problem

Brand pages will list venues stocking a brand, and Paweł predicted the reaction exactly: *"already hear b2b owners complaining 'why in brand X is sorting like that? I do not appear there!'"* Selling position is the obvious way to make money from a directory, it is available at any moment, and the pressure to do it will be highest when revenue is worst — which is the worst moment to be deciding it.

## Options considered

Sell position on brand, city and district pages. Sell inclusion. Offer paid "featured" slots visually separated from organic results. Never sell any of it, and instead make the ranking rule visible so owners can act on it.

## Decision

**Position, inclusion and filter outcomes are never for sale, and we say so on the site.** Revenue comes from selling a venue honest information about its own visibility — a service that helps them and misleads nobody. The complaint Paweł predicted is real, but it is not "your sort is wrong"; it is **"I cannot see why I am below them"**, and that is answered by transparency rather than by a price list.

## Rules

Default sort is by breadth, and the page states the rule in one line so an owner can read it and act on it — this is a visible count, not a formula weight, so `credibility-formula-opacity.md` does not apply. Alternative sorts stay available, since an owner who is fourth by breadth is often first by proximity. No paid placement, no paid inclusion, no sponsored slots, and no brand-funded feature. If a venue asks how to rank higher, the answer is always an action they can take on their own offer.

## What this prevents

Every number in the owner report becoming suspect at once. The product's only real asset is that its figures can survive the question "how do you know?" — and a purchasable ranking makes the honest answer unavailable for all of them, not just for position. It also prevents the slower failure: paid ranking would put UNI's revenue in direct conflict with the user's choice, which is the one thing [[decisions/product/mission-is-the-healthy-choice]] rules out.

## Revisit when

Never for ranking. Advertising that is clearly separated from results and not sold to alcohol brands is a different question, and one that should be asked on its own rather than as an erosion of this.
