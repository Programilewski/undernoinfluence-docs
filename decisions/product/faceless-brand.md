# Faceless brand — platform as authority, no personal branding

**Date:** 2026-04-22
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** standing rule
**Superseded:** in part, 2026-09-10 (Instagram) and 2026-09-18 (TikTok) by Paweł's growth plan — the ban on platforms is lifted; the ban on a face, personal content and lifestyle content holds on every platform. See [[decisions/adr/ADR-007 No Social Media]]
**Area:** Brand

---

## Problem

Building a new platform in a niche community is hard. The fastest shortcut is founder-led personal branding: a face, a newsletter, a social media presence that builds an audience and funnels them to the product. It works — but it creates a dependency. If the founder's personal brand becomes the growth engine, the platform's credibility is tied to a person, not to the data. That's the wrong foundation for an authority-based directory.

## Options considered

**Founder-led personal branding** — founder posts content about NoLo scene, builds following, promotes UNI through personal channels. Fast early traction. Platform credibility = founder credibility. Fragile long-term.

**Lifestyle brand / community approach** — UNI positions as a sober-curious community hub with soft aesthetics, user stories, blog content. Familiar category, crowded, wrong positioning.

**Faceless data authority** — no founder face in the product, no personal social accounts driving traffic, no lifestyle content. Platform earns trust through data quality alone. Growth via SEO, word-of-mouth, and B2B owner referrals.

## Decision

UNI is faceless. No personal branding, no founder social accounts connected to UNI, no lifestyle content. The platform is the authority — it earns trust because the data is accurate, not because someone endorsable stands behind it. Venue owners promote UNI because it makes them look good, not because they're fans of the person who built it.

## Rules

No founder name or photo in any B2C surface. ~~No UNI-branded social media accounts on platforms that require personal-style content (Instagram, TikTok).~~ **Since 10.09 and 18.09, UNI runs brand accounts on Instagram and TikTok. What is banned is a register, not a platform: no founder face or voice, no personal content, no lifestyle content — anywhere.** The content is the data: which venues pour what, where, and how recently it was checked. On TikTok that means the map, the counts and screen recordings — never a person to camera. Brand tone is authoritative and informational, not personal or conversational. Marketing materials reference the platform's data quality and methodology, not the team behind it. The "Under No ___" campaign concept is brand-led, not person-led.

## What this prevents

Prevents the single-point-of-failure problem: a founder's health issue, controversy, or pivot interest can collapse a personal-brand-dependent product overnight. Prevents the wrong brand associations — a wellness influencer aesthetic or sober-curious activist positioning would attract the wrong audience and repel venues that don't want to be political. Keeps the trust model clean: users trust UNI because the data is verified, not because they like the founder's vibe.

## Revisit when

Post-V1, if growth is significantly slower than projected and qualitative research shows that trust is the blocker (not awareness). At that point, consider a "methodology transparency" page that explains who built it and why — not personal branding, but accountability. Never return to founder-led social growth as the primary channel.
