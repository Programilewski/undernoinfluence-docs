# No paid legal opinion in V1

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** standing rule — the KCPU e-mail was sent 18.10.2026; no reply by 26.10. Waiting on it is in the After launch backlog
**Area:** Strategy | Brand

---

## Problem

The alcohol-advertising bill the Sejm passed on 08.10.2026 reaches non-alcoholic drinks sold under an alcohol brand. The 18.10 commute briefing (v14.0, Q2) recommended one paid opinion from a lawyer specialising in alcohol advertising, about 1–2k zł, once the Senate's version is known. V1 is zero-cost market research: UNI earns nothing, and the rule for V1 spending is no money on anything that hasn't been validated.

## Options considered

Pay for one opinion after the Senate text is known (the briefing's recommendation). Pay nothing, and take the risk out of the product instead. Ask the state body for free.

## Decision

**No paid legal consultation while UNI earns nothing.** Decided 19.10 in a claude.ai chat, following the same day's decision to strip brands from every public drink label instead of waiting for a legal review. Removing the exposure costs nothing; buying an opinion on it costs money V1 doesn't spend. This answers Q2 in the 18.10 briefing.

**The free alternative is an e-mail to KCPU** asking how the rule reads for a directory like UNI. Paweł sent it on 18.10, the day before this decision, asking about the current law and the amendment. It asks four questions: whether naming a 0.0 drink sold under an alcohol trademark on a venue profile is advertising; whether purely factual information about low-alcohol drinks from the venue's menu is allowed; whether social posts showing several venues, with no alcohol brands, can fall under the ban; and whether selling producers aggregated, anonymous interest data affects that assessment. It asks KCPU to name the competent body if the questions aren't theirs.

**What it didn't ask:** the grey area left open by the shared-recipes record, i.e. whether a recipe named after a cocktail ("Mojito bezalkoholowe") counts under the bill's *podobieństwo* (similarity) wording, and whether generic style words ("lager", "gin 0%") do. Both are for a follow-up. The low-alcohol question describes drinks UNI refuses to list ([[decisions/product/catalogue-excludes-actual-alcohol]]), and the e-mail's description of the service mentions "niskoalkoholowe"; a follow-up can correct that, so the answer is about the service UNI actually is.

## Rules

No paid legal work on alcohol advertising in V1. The KCPU e-mail asks a question; it states no position on the bill or on alcohol policy, in line with the mission. Its answer is guidance, not a binding ruling. KCPU is the body whose October 2025 guide for prosecutors reads "advertising" as widely as it can, so expect the strict reading, and don't treat silence as permission.

## What this prevents

Spending money UNI doesn't earn on a question the product can avoid instead. It also stops "wait for the lawyer" from holding up the launch, which is due in early December for Suchy Styczeń 2027.

## Revisit when

Before the first monetised feature or the first paying venue, whichever comes first. A paying customer changes both the stakes and the budget.
