# The brand reaches beyond the app only after the app and social media have traction

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Status:** Decided
**Executed:** not applicable — a sequencing decision; the parked ideas below are not yet written into the growth strategy
**Area:** Brand

---

## Problem

UNI should become more than an app — a brand people recognise for the healthy choice. Partnerships with existing groups, a window sticker on venues, a data report for the press and a newsletter would all spread it. But each one asks a stranger to trust UNI, and a site with no visible track record can't earn that trust. Outreach done too early is rejected, and it also takes the founder's time from the venues and social channels that create the track record.

## Options considered

Start outreach now, in parallel with the launch. Do nothing beyond the app. Build traction through the app and Instagram/TikTok first, and keep the outreach ideas written down and ready for when UNI is known.

## Decision

Traction comes first: the app and the faceless Instagram and TikTok accounts. Partnerships, the sticker and the other ideas wait until UNI is "somewhat known". In the founder's words: *nobody trusts a website created yesterday.* The ideas are kept ready rather than dropped, each with what would trigger it.

## Rules

The parked ideas are a twice-yearly data report for the press, a criteria-based window sticker (earned against a published standard, never paid for), partnerships with running clubs, sports groups and similar communities that already order alcohol-free, and a newsletter generated from the data. Later ones: annual awards from data and member votes, "swap one" nights hosted by venues, and data for city guides. None of them uses a founder face, an influencer, lifestyle content or producer sponsorship. Recovery and abstinence organisations are never a recruitment channel. When an idea is picked up, its trigger is stated as something measurable, not a feeling.

## What this prevents

It prevents spending trust UNI doesn't have yet: a sticker nobody recognises, or a partnership request from a brand with no audience, gets refused and is harder to ask again. It also keeps the founder's time on the venues, which are still what moves the launch.

## Revisit when

The app and social accounts show sustained traction. The thresholds are still to be set, for example organic visits a month, followers, or the first venue asking to be listed without being approached.
