# ADR-007: No Social Media

## Status
**Superseded.** Originally decided: systems-only growth.
**Executed:** superseded — see below
**Superseded:** 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-18 by Paweł's growth plan — **UNI runs faceless brand accounts on Instagram (kept 10.09) and TikTok (added 18.09).** The ban on a face and on personal content moved to [[decisions/product/faceless-brand]], which now carries it on every platform.

**The cost this record named is accepted, not refuted.** Carousels and short videos are manual work that stops when you stop — exactly the objection below. The plan keeps them anyway because search and owner sharing take months to compound, and a data-led account is the only channel that can show the catalogue to people before either does. The automation path this record wanted still applies: month one by hand, then generated from the database if the format works.

**One platform risk to watch on TikTok.** Its Community Guidelines (effective 13.09.2025) do not allow "trading, marketing, or providing access to" alcohol from ordinary accounts, and in some regions keep "showing adults having or consuming alcohol" out of the For You feed. They say nothing either way about 0.0% drinks. Moderation is largely automated and a 0.0% beer carries an alcohol brand's label, so posts built on bottles and drinking may be reach-limited as if they were alcohol. Formats built on the map, the counts and screen recordings avoid the question; check the reach of the first posts before committing to a format.

*The original record follows, unchanged.*

## Decision
No Instagram, TikTok, or social media content creation. All growth channels must be systems that run without ongoing human effort.

## Rationale
- Social media requires constant human input and dies the moment you stop
- It conflicts with the "build standalone systems, not personal brands" philosophy
- Faceless brand by design — no personality to build social presence around
- SEO auto-generates from data, venue sharing is owner-driven, physical cards are one-time effort
- Solo founder time is better spent on product than content calendars

## What Replaces Social
- [[business/Growth Strategy#SEO]] — pages auto-generate, compound over time
- [[business/Growth Strategy#Venue-Driven Sharing]] — owners promote their own profiles
- [[business/Growth Strategy#Physical Touchpoints]] — table cards, event flyers

## When to Revisit
If all system-based channels plateau and growth stalls. Even then, consider hiring someone for social rather than doing it personally.
