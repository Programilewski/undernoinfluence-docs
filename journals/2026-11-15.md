### 2. What the first real round on the home server showed

Five runs in total. Three failed at once because the Brave key wasn't there yet (no request was sent, so they cost nothing). Then one query for Śródmieście, then all seven templates for Śródmieście: **8 Brave requests, 75 domains, 17 candidates.** Every candidate's menu was looked for, 12 menus were read twice with Haiku, and 65 drink lines came out.

**By template** (candidates that are real venues): `"0,0%" piwo menu restauracja` 3 of 3 (Taproom, Moffo, The Chef House, and the richest menus); `"piwo bezalkoholowe" … filetype:pdf` 3 of 3; `intitle:menu "bezalkoholowe"` 2 of 2; `mocktail menu … filetype:pdf` 2 of 3; mojito 1 of 2; **`"wino bezalkoholowe" karta` 0 of 4, all shops**; `koktajle … filetype:pdf` nothing.

**Cost:** $0.048 for 12 menus, about $0.004 a menu, the same as measured on 11.11. The five "menus" that were shop pages cost $0.006 between them.

**What worked:** scanned PDF pages with broken text were read as images (Czerwony Wieprz: 3 of 4 pages); unbranded lines were dropped (Cucina Povera's only "piwo bezalkoholowe"); soft drinks and "Zero" colas were left out; where the two readings disagreed about scope (Hardmade ice tea, a banana "mocktail"), the line was kept for a person; Moffo's Aperol Spritz 0% was flagged for its "aperitivo"; "MOJITO VIRGIN" matched the shared mojito recipe with certainty.

### 3. Five fixes, shipped as v0.1.10

Two application commits, both pushed, tagged **v0.1.10** and deployed to the home server. Tests: the sourcing suites (203 tests) green on the PC; CI green before the deploy.

- **An online shop is not a venue.** Five of the 17 candidates were shops (iconselection, beztrosko, Republika Wina, FineSpirits, herbataikawa): each had a Warsaw address, opening hours and a wine word, so the venue check passed them. The model reading their pages said "this is an online shop" every time, and nothing acted on it. The check now fails a homepage with a shop's own words ("dodaj do koszyka", "add to cart", "regulamin sklepu"), a Shopify storefront, or a schema.org shop type. A restaurant/bar/café/hotel type still wins, and the word "koszyk" alone doesn't count, because Semolino's homepage has it (online ordering, and "Hala Koszyki"). **Checked on the round's 17 real homepages:** all five shops and Dom Whisky (a whisky shop whose bar is in Gdańsk) now fail; all eleven venues still pass. **Why it matters:** each false candidate costs Paweł a look, and the 80% target was being missed mainly because of shops.
- **A drink without a printed price counts.** Taproom Wilanów's 25 non-alcoholic beers come from an Untappd tap list with no prices, so under the 11.11 rule the best venue of the round didn't qualify. Paweł decided: no price requirement, anywhere. The price is still recorded and shown as information; "Bez ceny w karcie" is gone from the rejection reasons. Recorded as [[decisions/product/a-drink-without-a-price-still-counts]], and test 2 of [[decisions/product/what-belongs-in-the-catalogue]] is amended.
- **Only real disagreements block one-click approval.** 33 of the 65 lines were marked "disputed", and 20 of those only differed in the section heading or the model's brand/product guess ("Koktajle bezalkoholowe / Mocktails" against "Koktajle bezalkoholoweMocktails"; "Viva La Vie Merlot 0%" against "Merlot 0% Viva La Vie"). A disputed line never goes through *Zatwierdź pewne*, so all of Moffo needed clicking one by one. The two readings are now compared only on what changes a decision: kind, house-drink base, category, a house drink's public name, and strength.
- **A size printed without its unit no longer splits a drink in two.** Czerwony Wieprz's Lech Free came out as two lines each: one reading wrote "0,33l", the other "0,33" (and the menu's misprint "0%,33"). The two didn't pair, and the copies got different catalogue matches. Once a name has stated its strength with "%", a bare decimal after it is now read as the bottle size. A bare "0.0" or "0,5" with no "%" before it is still a strength, so "Heineken 0.0" is untouched.
- **A branded beer is never suggested a cocktail recipe.** "Żywiec Bezalkoholowy", "Lager Bezalkoholowe" and "Czarny Bez Bezalkoholowe" were suggested "Mule bezalkoholowy", "Spritz bezalkoholowy" and "Mojito bezalkoholowe": the only thing in common was the word. A shared recipe is now only ever a house drink's match.

`database/schema.md` was updated in the same commits (`has_price` is information only; which differences count as a disagreement; shops counted under "Nie lokal").

## Open Questions

1. **Should a query already sent be skipped?** Today, no: within one run a repeated query is sent once, but across runs nothing is checked. On 15.11 the second run re-sent `intitle:menu "bezalkoholowe" Śródmieście Warszawa`, which the run before it had just sent, for 1 wasted request of 8. Brave's terms allow no storing of results, so the saving can only come from not sending. **Recommendation:** skip a query sent successfully in the last 30 days, show "pominięto N" in the run, and offer a "search again anyway" switch, because new venues appear and a month is long enough for results to change. Your call on the window. **Answered 15.11, after the wrap:** yes, 30 days, built first thing next session.

## Business Impact

The pipeline is what replaces hand-typing the ~30 launch venues, and today was its first measured round on real venues outside the PC. Fewer false disagreements means one click instead of twenty for a clean menu, which is the difference between the admin time scaling or not.

## Learning Path

- **Why a cached config needs rebuilding after `.env` changes.** Laravel reads `.env` once and caches the result for speed; until the cache is rebuilt, a new key doesn't exist for the app. Search "Laravel config cache env production".
- **Incus containers and Tailscale devices.** A container that runs Tailscale itself becomes a device in your tailnet; deleting the container doesn't remove the device. Search "Tailscale remove machine from tailnet".
- **Precision on real data.** The shop rule was written after looking at what the 17 real homepages contain, then checked on all of them: the same method as the 13.11 venue check. Search "rule-based classifier precision recall evaluation".
- **Normalisation before comparing.** Two readings of one menu write the same drink differently; normalising names (case, accents, sizes) is what lets them pair, and normalising too much is what turns a strength into a size. Search "string normalization record linkage".
