## What Was Built

All in the application repository, tagged **v0.1.21** (CI green) and deployed to the home server.

- **A strength printed beside a line vouches for it.** `MenuLine::menuClaimsStrengthOf` now reads the printed strength (`abv_as_printed`) alongside the printed name and the heading. `MenuItemName` gained `claimsAlcoholFreeInWordsOnly` and `printedWithinLimit`, so a printed 0,5% or less satisfies a product name that says alcohol-free in words ("Bezalkoholowe", "Non-Alcoholic"), and never one that states "0,0%". The refusal message names the printed strength too. **Why:** Taproom's tap list prints "Mała Czarna", heading "IPA 35", strength "0,0%" in a column. The guard saw no strength and refused three real alcohol-free beers. As with headings, the spelling is not kept as an alias. Record: [[decisions/product/a-strength-printed-beside-a-line-vouches-for-it]].
- **Otwórz menu opens the venue's own menu.** New `MenuSource::originalUrl()` (the file, else the page) and `homepageUrl()` (the venue's site, else the candidate's, else the menu's address, stripped to the domain).
  - **On the menu page:** "Otwórz menu" (Ctrl+Shift+O) opens the original, "Kopia w UNI" opens the stored copy, and "Strona lokalu" opens the homepage.
  - **In Przegląd pozycji:** **O** opens the original, **W** the homepage, and **M** the menu's page in UNI (what O used to do).

  **Why:** Kamanda Lwowska's stored copy was a JavaScript splash screen ("0%"), and Paweł had to find the real menu himself. Record: [[decisions/product/otworz-menu-opens-the-venues-own-menu]].
- **A house drink that names a catalogue product is suggested the product.**
  - `MenuItemResolver::productsNamedIn()` finds products whose name, strength words aside (`MenuItemName::withoutStrength`), is printed whole in a line: never part of a word, at least four letters, and never a shared recipe.
  - `MenuLineMatcher` turns such a house-drink line into "Sugestie" when the menu backs the product's strength.
  - "Popraw" then opens on the product, and correcting to a product now records the line's kind as a product.

  **Why:** "MARTINI VIBRANTE 300 ml" was approved as a house drink although the catalogue had Martini Vibrante 0%. Record: [[decisions/product/a-house-drink-that-names-a-product-is-suggested-the-product]].
- **Tests:** new cases in `DecideMenuLineActionTest`, `AdminFasterApprovalTest`, `AdminMenuApprovalTest` and `MenuLineMatcherTest`. Three older tests now state "no printed strength" explicitly; they had silently inherited the factory's "0,0%".
- **Home-server data (test data):** line 366 (Martini Vibrante at Restauracja Navona) went back to the queue. Its house drink was removed and the line was re-matched, so it now waits as a suggestion for Paweł to confirm with C.

## Business Impact

At about 4 minutes per venue on hard menus, and seconds per line on known ones, the ~30 launch venues are around two hours of approval work, and the hard part has moved. Identifying unknown drinks is now about half as costly with AI, and each product created makes the next menu faster. What's left is overhead per venue: creating it (about 40 s) and throwing out sites that aren't venues (about 50 s each). The next speed-ups should come from the sourcing side (fewer non-venues reaching reading), not from approval. The house-drink suggestion matters beyond speed: a product served mixed (Martini Vibrante with tonic) now counts as a place to find that product, which is what a person searching for it needs.

## Learning Path

- **Server-rendered vs JavaScript-rendered pages.** Why a saved copy of some sites shows only a splash screen: the content is drawn by scripts after the page loads. Search "client-side rendering vs server-side rendering".
- **Tabular menus and data extraction.** A tap list is a table (name, style, strength) rather than a sentence; reading it means keeping each value tied to its row. Search "table extraction from PDFs and web pages".
