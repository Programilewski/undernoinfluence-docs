## What Was Built

### EH2 — Analytics failures now reported to exception handler

`AnalyticsFailureReporter::report()` previously only logged a `warning`. The audit required it to also call `report($throwable)` so exceptions surface in Sentry/Bugsnag rather than silently disappearing. This one-line addition was already made in the previous session, but caused two tests to fail because those tests used `Log::shouldReceive('warning')` partial mocks, and `report()` internally calls `Log::error()` through Laravel's exception handler — an unexpected call the mocks rejected.

**Fix:** Added `Log::shouldReceive('error')->zeroOrMoreTimes()` to both failing tests to acknowledge the new behavior without constraining it. This documents the contract (reporter both warns and escalates) rather than hiding it.

Tests affected:
- `AuditLoggingTest::test_analytics_failure_reporter_logs_context_without_throwing`
- `ValidationAndApiRegressionTest::test_analytics_failure_reporter_reports_gateway_exception_without_failing_response`
