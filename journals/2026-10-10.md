
# 2026-10-10 — Crash courses for the trip

## Session Overview

**Goal:** you're away for six days (10–16.10), with time on flights and at the destination, and asked for reading material: crash courses on the most important things you might not know enough about, with the choice of topics left to me.

**Outcome:** nine crash courses and a start-here page in `learning/`, about 18 500 words (roughly five and a half hours of reading). ## Decision Register

### 1. The crash courses live in `learning/`, as Markdown, synced to the phone — **permanent**

**Chosen:** Markdown files in the workspace's `learning/` folder, which Syncthing carries to the phone's Obsidian vault for offline reading on the plane. **Rejected:** a published web page or an online document (needs a connection in flight); a PDF/EPUB export (one more file to move to the phone, and no place in the workspace's index or links).

### 2. Courses teach; they don't reopen decisions — **permanent**

Where a course touches a decided record (block-all-ai-crawlers, raw-events-are-kept-not-pruned, 404 for every non-live venue), it explains the record and puts anything worth reconsidering into "Questions to bring back". **Rejected:** arguing for changes inside the teaching text, which would mix learning with lobbying.

### 3. Courses 7–9 complement the line-by-line Ansible and Deployer docs, not replace them — **permanent**

**Chosen:** mental models, failure reasoning and the end-to-end cycle, pointing to `ansible-explained.md` and `deployer-explained.md` for every detail. **Rejected:** rewriting those two documents now. They're thorough, and only lightly out of date (see the debt log).
