### The sourcing pipeline, step 3, commits 1 and 2

| Commit | What it does, in plain words | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — fetching** | `php artisan uni:fetch {url}` fetches one menu as "UNIbot" and files it under the venue's candidate. It refuses to connect anywhere but a public web address (checked again at every redirect, so a link cannot make the server read its own internals); it obeys robots.txt and all three "do not mine my text" signals; one request per page, 25 MB at most. Only the current menu file is kept, privately; a newer one replaces it and the old row keeps its address, fingerprint and dates. Deleting a venue deletes its files; erasing a venue turns its candidate into a permanent "no", so it is never proposed again | `app/Services/Sourcing/*`, `FetchMenu` command, `SourcingCandidate`, `MenuSource`, two enums, two migrations, `config/uni.php` → `sourcing`, `schema.md` |
| **2 — text** | Turns the file into what the model will read: text where the PDF's text can be trusted, a picture of the page where it can't (too short, wrong code page, letters interleaved — two real menus fooled the prototype this way). HTML pages become their plain text. Very long screenshots are cut into pieces. Repeated headers/footers are dropped, but never a line with a number, so no price is lost (the prototype lost one). Ansible now installs the PDF tools on the servers | `MenuTextExtractor`, `TextQuality`, `HtmlText`, `MenuText`; Ansible php and verify roles |

Both were checked on real menus (Semolino's PDF: 25,796 characters; Tygmont's page: 5,819). The tests build their own PDFs and fake DNS and the web; no real menu is a fixture.

## Learning Path

- **Storage limitation (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e)).** Why "until decided" is not an end date. Search "GDPR storage limitation principle".
- **SSRF — server-side request forgery.** Why a fetcher must refuse private addresses and re-check every redirect. Search "OWASP SSRF prevention cheat sheet" and "DNS rebinding".
- **robots.txt, RFC 9309.** Groups, longest match, and what a 4xx/5xx means. Search "RFC 9309 robots exclusion protocol".
- **TDMRep.** The W3C community protocol for reserving text-and-data mining: `tdmrep.json`, the header and the meta tag. Search "TDMRep W3C".
- **Mojibake.** Why "pi³ka" means Windows-1250 read as Latin-1. Search "mojibake Windows-1250".
- **Model disagreement as a review signal.** Two runs that differ mark where to look; agreement isn't proof. Search "self-consistency LLM".
