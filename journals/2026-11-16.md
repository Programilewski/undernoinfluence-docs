### 1. Skipping Brave queries already sent (decided 15.11)

- **What:** a search leaves out every query a run sent successfully in the last 30 days (`uni.sourcing.search.skip_sent_within_days`). The form shows how many will be skipped; a **"Szukaj mimo to"** switch sends them anyway. The notification and a new **"Pominięte"** column in Wyszukiwania show the count. Skipped queries don't use up the 30-per-run limit; a run with nothing left finishes without a job.
- **How "sent successfully" is known:** a run sends its queries in order and stops at the first error, so its sent queries are its first `requests`. A query that failed is not skipped.
- **Why:** Brave's terms forbid storing results, so the only saving is not sending. The home server's batch proved the size of it: 31 of 68 requests were repeats, and run 7 (30 requests) produced **0 new candidates**.
- **Where:** `SearchForVenuesAction::start/recentlySent`, `ListSourcingCandidates`, `SourcingSearchResource`, column `sourcing_searches.skipped`.

### 2. A district × category page needs three venues to be indexed

- **What:** `/warszawa/mokotow/piwo-bezalkoholowe` with fewer than 3 venues (counted as the page counts them: a fresh drink of that category) is served with `noindex, follow` and left out of the sitemap. Its own setting: `uni.min_venues_for_indexable_district_category`.
- **Why:** it indexed from one venue while the district page above it needed three. With the pipeline adding venues in bulk, pages multiplied by district × category with one or two entries are the shape Google's scaled-content policy describes. The config itself marked this as deliberately open; Paweł's "do all points" closed it. The thin-district record is amended.
- **Where:** `config/uni.php`, `city/district-category.blade.php`, `SitemapController`, `SeoLandingPageTest`.

### 4. Source B: the permit register (ADR-012)

- **The file:** `items.csv`, 2,825 places (Typ = Gastronomia, confirmed by Paweł), one row per place with its permits merged: `punkt, adres, rodzaje, wazne_od, wazne_do, ze_ids`. **No owner, firm or NIP**, so nothing personal enters.
- **"Wczytaj listę zezwoleń"** on Kandydaci: each named place becomes a `licence_list` candidate with its name, street, number, the register's description and its permit ids. On the full file: **2,615 candidates, 138 excluded** (stalls, banquet and conference rooms, theatre and cinema buffets), **73 without a name**. A place already known by any of its ids is not added again, so next month's list adds only new places and a rejection stays one; renewed permits' ids are added to the place. The uploaded file is deleted once read.
- **"Znajdź strony lokali (Brave)":** up to 30 places by kind (bars first), one query each (`"{name}" {street} Warszawa`), the 30-day skip applies. Of up to 8 result domains, **those carrying a word of the name are checked first**; the first whose homepage or contact page gives **the name and the register's street and number** becomes the site, and its menu is looked for at once (free). A site another candidate holds is left to it (probably a chain). Nothing of Brave's results is stored: a place not found keeps only "none of N sites gave the name and address".
- **The test batch on the PC** (20 bars, $0.10, then the 15 misses again for $0.075): first **5 of 20** sites. Why: Backroom's own site came **16th** in its results, after directories, review sites and company registers, while only 5 domains were checked; and Hala Koszyki's site gave a tenant's address, so it claimed Bibimbar. After the fixes (name-first ranking, up to 8, name required, 23 directories blocked): **7 of 20**. Four of the rest had only Facebook/Tripadvisor results: probably no site of their own, so they can't be listed anyway (a web address is required). One chain end to end: register row → gringobar.pl → menu PDF → read with page images → "CORONA 0%" in the queue.
- **Where:** `LicenceList` (parsing, name, address, page checks), `ImportLicenceListAction`, `FindVenueSiteAction`, jobs `FindVenueSites` and `VerifyVenueSite`, `SourcingDomainVerifier::homeAndContact` (now shared with source A), Kandydaci's table (name with address, "Strona" column, "Strona lokalu" filter). ### 5. Five approval speed-ups (Paweł asked for all five)

1. **"Utwórz produkt" on a line:** one form pre-filled from the reading (brand, product, category, optional style). The brand is found ignoring capitals or created; the product is created with **strength unknown** (a menu's "0,0 %" is not a verified strength); the printed spelling becomes an alias; the line is approved as the product when its menu has a venue. A name claiming a strength the menu doesn't print is refused, as in "Popraw". Why: 43 of the batch's 98 lines were brands the catalogue lacked, and adding one meant leaving the menu.
2. **Automatic re-matching:** saving a brand, a product or a confirmed spelling queues one re-match of every waiting line on every menu (`CatalogueObserver` → `RematchPendingMenuLines`). Adding Bavaria once settles all 7 Bavaria lines already read. Decided lines are never touched.
3. **Across menus:** Pozycje z menu has a **"Gotowe do zatwierdzenia"** filter and a bulk **"Zatwierdź"** that approves only clean lines (certain or house drink, a venue, readings agreeing, no alcoholic ingredient) and skips the rest. Biblioteka menu has a bulk **"Utwórz lokale"** for menus whose candidate has a name, street and number, with one venue type for the batch.
4. **"Przegląd pozycji"** (button on Pozycje z menu): every waiting line, one at a time, menus with a venue first, on single keys: **A** zatwierdź, **C** popraw, **P** utwórz produkt, **R** odrzuć, **S** pomiń, **G** Google, **O** strona menu. After a decision the next line comes up; a refused one stays.
5. **The line in its place:** the review shows the menu's own text around the name, highlighted, and the lines printed around it, so the file rarely needs opening.

## Learning Path

- **Why "skip, don't cache".** A search API's terms can forbid storing results; then the only legal saving is not repeating the request. Search "Brave Search API storage rights terms".
- **Record linkage with two keys.** A food hall shares one address with many places; requiring the name and the address together is what makes a match. Search "record linkage blocking keys address name".
- **Observers and queued jobs.** Saving a product triggers an event; a queued, de-duplicated job re-matches the waiting lines without slowing the save. Search "Laravel model observers ShouldBeUnique job".
