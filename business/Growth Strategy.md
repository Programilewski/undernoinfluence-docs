# Growth Strategy

> Core philosophy: every growth channel is a system that runs without ongoing human effort. No personal branding, no content calendars, no channels that die when you stop feeding them.

## SEO — Primary Channel
- Auto-generate landing pages from venue database: one per city + category (e.g., "Najlepsze bezalkoholowe piwo w Warszawie")
- Each venue profile is its own indexable page
- SEO surface grows automatically as database grows
- Low competition — nobody owns structured NoLo search in Polish yet
- Requires content density to rank — thin pages with 5 venues won't outrank blog posts
- This is a system: more venues → more pages → more traffic → more users → more venues

## Venue-Driven Sharing — Product as Growth Channel
- Make venue profiles useful enough that owners share them on their own social media
- Shareable profile links, potential "NoLo menu powered by UNI" badges for venue websites
- Each owner sharing is free, targeted distribution to their existing audience
- This is a system: better profiles → more sharing → more user acquisition

## Physical Touchpoints — Place Once, Work Indefinitely
- Table cards/stickers at partner venues: "Looking for more places like this? Scan here."
- QR code deep links to the hosting venue's profile first — venue gets first impression
- Value exchange: "Put our card on tables, get free premium analytics for 6 months"
- NoLo events, tastings, sober-curious meetups — QR flyers
- Avoid: random street banners, bar bathroom posters — near-zero conversion
- This is a system: one-time placement, compounding returns

## Expansion Strategy
- Warsaw only for first 3-6 months minimum
- One city with 80+ venues beats 5 cities with 15 half-empty listings
- Users get one first impression per city — sparse data = they never come back
- Document onboarding playbook in Warsaw, replicate city by city
- Warsaw SEO rankings and venue testimonials = proof for next cities

## The Cold Start Playbook
1. CEIDG/KRS → prospecting list of Warsaw bars and restaurants
2. Manual enrichment (websites, social media, Untappd, a visit — **not phone calls**, 21.09)
3. Contact NoLo distributors for pre-qualified venue lists
4. Onboard 50-80 venues with meaningful NoLo offerings
5. SEO pages go live, organic traffic begins
6. Table cards at onboarded venues drive user acquisition
7. User activity generates analytics data → B2B value proposition strengthens

## Explicitly NOT Doing
- Social media content creation (requires constant human input, dies when you stop)
- Personal branding (brand is faceless by design)
- Paid ads (premature before product-market fit)
- Blog (no content creation, SEO pages auto-generate from data)
