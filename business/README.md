# Business Documentation

Business source of truth for the B2C/B2B marketplace in Poland.

## Documents

- `model.md` - business model, customer segments, value proposition, and revenue logic.
- `pricing.md` - pricing assumptions and packaging.
- `metrics.md` - business KPIs and reporting definitions.
- `competitors.md` - competitor and positioning notes.

## Rules

- Keep assumptions explicit.
- Link analytics definitions to `../tech/analytics.md`.
- Link monetization flows that involve personal data to `../compliance/ropa.md`.
