1 · Brand at a glance

Under No Influence helps people discover bars, restaurants and cafés with a genuine alcohol-free offer. The brand is confident, modern and a little defiant — the name itself is the statement. Visually: clean high-contrast surfaces, one decisive violet, and a single signature gesture, the emphasised NO.

2 · Logo

The primary logo is a two-line wordmark in custom letterforms, delivered as inline SVG (not a font). The fragment NO. is set in accent violet; the rest uses currentColor, so the mark inherits the surrounding text colour and works in both modes from a single asset.

5 · Dark mode

Tokens flip on .dark applied to <html> before first paint — no flash.

  • Brand violet & drink palette stay identical in both modes
  • Never hard-code #fff/#000 — use tokens
  • Test every new component in both modes

6 · Voice & tone

  • Confident, not preachy — we help people choose, we don't lecture
  • Plain & specific — concrete counts beat vague marketing
  • Polish-first — UI in Polish, Polish category names
  • The name does the attitude — let NO. carry the edge
