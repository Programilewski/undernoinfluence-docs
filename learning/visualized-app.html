One marketplace, three operating domains
Public discovery creates demand signals. Admin supply operations turn messy venue data into public inventory. B2B owners claim, maintain, and monetize their profiles through freshness and analytics.
Public gate: is_active=true Hidden gate: is_canary=false Supply state: draft -> ready -> published Owner state: claim pending -> approved -> owner panel
End user
Visitors discover no/low alcohol venues, inspect profiles, take outbound actions, save places, and report bad data.
Admin
Internal operators import supply, clean locations, moderate claims/products, publish venues, and handle compliance.
B2B venue owner
Owners claim a venue, manage profile and offer data, keep freshness current, and read analytics gated by plan/features.
Shared data and policy spine
The same Venue, Product, User, Claim, Analytics, Audit, and external geocoding data powers all three domains.

Entry points

Public pages and SEO

Home, city, category, district-category, sitemap, and static trust pages funnel visitors into discovery or profiles.

//{citySlug}/kategoria/{seo_slug}/mapa
HomeController, CityController, CategoryController, SitemapController, PageController

Discovery

Map, filters, ranking

Visitors search by city, district, category, verified status, strict zero ABV, freshness, bounds, and nearest/best sorting.

  • Active venues only: active and not canary.
  • Map output is capped and normalized for markers.
  • Default sort favors breadth and category diversity.
Livewire\DiscoveryPage -> Discovery\Filters -> VenueQuery -> MapData

Venue decision

Profile inspection

Users inspect venue address, district, categories, products, drinks, freshness, nearby venues, and public contact links.

  • Profiles 404 unless active and not canary.
  • Nearby venues use haversine distance.
  • Freshness label hides on empty offer.
VenueController@show, Venue::formatted_address, Venue::freshness_label

Conversion signals

Outbound actions and saves

Directions, website, Instagram, menu/category view events, and saved venues become demand and engagement signals.

  • Redirects are throttled per IP, venue, and type.
  • Saved venues require auth and active public venue.
  • Analytics writes are isolated behind gateway/reporters.
VenueRedirectController, SaveVenueButton, AnalyticsEventController

Trust repair

Report inaccuracy

Users can report wrong venue data. Owners cannot report their own venue because the owner panel is the source of correction.

  • One pending report per venue/user flow.
  • Admin resolves through moderation resource.
VenueInaccuracyReportController, StoreVenueInaccuracyReportRequest

Claim CTA crosses into owner domain

Existing public venue or new venue submission begins the B2B verification path.

Supply acquisition

Import venues into queue

OSM/research CSV imports create draft supply with source, coordinates, notes, ambiguity flags, city/district, OSM ID, and seeding status.

  • Duplicates are skipped by OSM ID where available.
  • Rows can enter draft, ready, or skip depending quality.
venues:import-osm, venues:import-research-csv, ImportResearchCsv

Admin queue

Seeding queue QA workbench

Admins review draft/ready/skip venues, inspect mini-map and completeness, edit fields, and decide mark ready, skip, publish, or delete.

  • Bulk forward geocoding: GUS or Nominatim.
  • Bulk reverse geocoding: GUS or Nominatim.
  • Single-record tools support Google search and public preview.
SeedingQueueResource, EditSeedingQueue, SeedingQueueTable, VenueMiniMapWidget

Location enrichment

Geocoding and reverse geocoding

Street data can become coordinates, or coordinates can become address fields. GUS is batch-first; Nominatim is cached and rate-limited.

  • GUS forward: /api/fts/gc/pkt batch request.
  • GUS reverse: bbox-only /api/fts/ref/qq then closest point.
  • Nominatim: User-Agent, 30-day cache, 1.1s between uncached calls.
GusGeocodingService, NominatimGeocodingService, Geocode*/ReverseGeocode* actions

Publish gate

Ready -> Published -> Public

Publishing sets seeding status to published and is_active to true. Public controllers still block canary or inactive records.

  • Draft: still being cleaned.
  • Ready: human says it is publishable.
  • Skip: parked or intentionally excluded.
SeedingStatus enum, Venue::scopeActive(), VenueController@show

Catalog moderation

Products, brands, categories

Admins own the canonical catalog, ABV trust state, brand links, category taxonomy, and product proposal decisions.

  • Approved proposals create products and attach them to the venue.
  • Approval refreshes offer and may activate a venue.
ProductResource, BrandResource, CategoryResource, ApproveProductProposalAction

Claims

Owner verification

Admins approve/reject claim requests, record reviewer data, notify users, and promote approved users to owner role.

pendingapprovedrejected
VenueClaimResource, ApproveVenueClaimAction, RejectVenueClaimAction

Data quality

Reports and erasure

Admins process inaccuracy reports and GDPR-style venue erasure requests from email or owner self-service.

  • Erasure deletes venue, files, raw analytics, and redacts audit logs.
  • Owner role can be demoted if no claimed venues remain.
VenueInaccuracyReportResource, VenueErasureRequestResource, EraseVenueAction

Acquisition

Register and claim

A venue owner registers, verifies email, and submits a claim for an existing public venue or creates a new venue submission.

  • Existing claim requires active venue and unclaimed status.
  • New claim creates inactive draft venue from submitted details.
RegisterController, VenueClaimController, StoreVenueClaimRequest

Handoff

Approved claim

Approval marks venue claimed, assigns claimed_by, promotes user to owner, and sends the owner into the panel.

  • Approval currently sets venue inactive for review.
  • Owner can manage only venues scoped by claimed_by.

Owner panel

Profile and offer management

Owners update description/contact, attach catalog products, remove products, create custom drinks, and propose missing products.

  • Attach/detach updates freshness and scoring.
  • Proposal approval later creates canonical catalog product.
  • Profile updates are captured in PostHog.
Owner\VenueResource, ProductsRelationManager, DrinksRelationManager

Retention

Freshness and analytics

Owner widgets show profile status, analytics, freshness state, category coverage, saves, and paid insight modules by plan or override.

  • Free/Basic/Pro/Premium shape analytics visibility.
  • VenueFeatureFlag can override plan entitlements.
  • Freshness affects public trust and owner guidance.
VenueStatusWidget, VenueAnalyticsWidget, Venue::hasFeature(), VenueAnalyticsReportService

Exit

Erase venue and data

Owners can self-service delete a claimed venue and optionally delete their account.

  • Creates verified erasure request.
  • Completes immediately in owner flow.

Identity and access

Users, roles, auth gates

User role decides admin panel, owner panel, or public-only access. Email verification gates claim submission.

  • Roles: user, owner, admin.
  • Owner resources are scoped by claimed_by.
  • Admin has broad panel access and moderation powers.
UserRole, PanelAuthenticate, User::isAdmin(), User::isOwner()

Offer graph

Products, drinks, categories, scoring

Products and custom drinks create the user-facing offer, admin catalog workload, owner freshness workflow, and ranking/scoring signals.

  • venue_products pivot tracks availability, added_by, confirmed_at.
  • VenueProductObserver refreshes offer and recalculates score.
  • VenueScoring powers breadth ranking and analytics completeness.
Product, Brand, Category, VenueDrink, VenueScoring, VenueProductObserver

Telemetry

Analytics and reporting

Raw events and daily stats power admin dashboards, owner analytics, demand modules, freshness warnings, and privacy-sensitive reports.

  • VenueStat aggregates views, clicks, saves, shares.
  • AnalyticsEvent stores raw identifiable events with pruning.
  • Small bucket privacy avoids exposing weak signals.
DatabaseAnalyticsGateway, VenueAnalytics, DiscoveryAnalytics, PruneAnalyticsEvents

External services

Geocoding, PostHog, notifications

External systems enrich locations, record product analytics, and notify owners/users while app actions keep durable DB state authoritative.

  • GUS: official Polish address/reference data.
  • Nominatim: OSM search/reverse with cache and User-Agent.
  • Notifications: claim and erasure email outcomes.
GusGeocodingService, NominatimGeocodingService, PostHogService, Notifications

Audit and compliance

AuditLog + erasure

Moderation and destructive flows leave audit records; erasure redacts linked audit context and deletes files/raw events.

  • claim_submitted, claim_approved, product_proposal_* events.
  • Erasure request statuses: pending, verified, completed, rejected.
AuditLogger, AuditLog, VenueErasureRequest, EraseVenueAction
