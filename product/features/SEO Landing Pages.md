# SEO Landing Pages

**Status:** **Built.** **Verified against code 2026-08-18.** The cluster of `/{city}`, `/{city}/{district}`, `/{city}/{category}`, `/{city}/{district}/{category}` and `/kategoria/{slug}` pages is live; entry links were added 14.08 (before that, roughly 300 pages existed with not one link pointing at them). The sitemap is generated by `spatie/laravel-sitemap` and empty combinations get `noindex` ([[decisions/product/empty-landing-pages-noindex]]). One thing remains: these pages are currently empty or filled with generated venues, so their SEO value starts on the day real data arrives.

## Concept
Auto-generated pages from venue database, targeting Polish-language NoLo search queries. Zero manual content creation — pages update as venue data changes.

## URL Pattern
`/warszawa/piwo-bezalkoholowe`
`/warszawa/mocktaile`
`/warszawa/wino-bezalkoholowe`
`/krakow/piwo-bezalkoholowe` (future cities)

## Page Content
- H1: "Najlepsze bezalkoholowe piwo w Warszawie" (generated from city + category)
- Venue list filtered by city + category, ordered by credibility score
- Each venue shows: name, breadth bar for the relevant category, checkmark, product count
- Schema.org structured data (ItemList + LocalBusiness per venue)
- Internal links to venue profiles and other city/category pages

## SEO Mechanics
- Each page is indexable and targets a specific long-tail query
- Content density matters: pages with <5 venues likely won't rank above blog posts
- Another argument for Warsaw-first — build density before pages go live
- SEO surface grows automatically as venues are added
- Spatie Sitemap generates sitemap.xml including all SEO pages

## What Makes These Pages Rank
- Structured, unique data that Google can't get elsewhere
- Internal linking between related pages (piwo → wino → mocktaile in same city)
- Venue profiles as deep pages with their own structured data
- Page freshness signal from venue data updates (products added/confirmed)
