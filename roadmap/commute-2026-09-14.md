### The menu importer, in one screen

A CSV, one row per drink **as the menu writes it**: `venue` (slug), `drink` ("Heineken 0,0 but. 0,33"), `source_url`, `checked_at`, and `house_drink_type` for house drinks (`mocktail`, `koktajl_nolo`, `drink_spirytus_0`, `virgin_classic`).

- A row attaches **only** on a spelling you already confirmed or a unique exact product name — with its source and date, through the same follow-up as the manual button. A drink already on the menu is re-confirmed.
- Every other row fails with the closest products in the reason. **The failed-rows download is your review list**: add the spelling under *Pisownie z kart* on the right product, import those rows again. The spelling is remembered, and site search uses it too.
- **"0,0", "0%", "zero", "free", "bezalkoholowe" are never ignored.** A menu's plain "Heineken" can never become Heineken 0.0, and such a spelling cannot even be saved for it.

## Appendix B — the research behind today's changes, condensed

**Map, on a 390×844 phone, cold cache, CPU 4× slower.** This morning `/mapa` drew in 12.7 s on slow 4G and 46 s at 400 kbps, downloading ~2.2 MB: tiles 62%, library 19%, fonts 11%, icon sheet 6%. One central-Warsaw tile weighs 202 KB at zoom 9 and 39 KB at zoom 12, so the opening view decides most of the weight. Tonight on the built bundle: tapping Mapa to pins in 3.4 s on slow 4G (library prefetched) and 16.1 s at 400 kbps. The worker reuses the page's copy of the shared MapLibre module — 0 KB second download — **only when the server sends cache headers** (checklist C6). Sources: [MapLibre v6 migration guide](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/guides/v5-to-v6-migration-guide/) · [MDN on connection quality](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/NetworkInformation/effectiveType).

**Login.** Deleting `trustProxies` closed forged addresses completely; per-address limits stay a mitigation — an IPv6 customer holds at least a /64 ([adam-p.ca](https://adam-p.ca/blog/2022/02/ipv6-rate-limiting/), [CVE-2026-45364](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-p6v2-xcpg-h6xw)) and OWASP counts failures per account ([cheat sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authentication_Cheat_Sheet.html)). The plain `Host` header: Ploi's catch-all site ([roadmap](https://roadmap.ploi.io/projects/1-server-level-requests/items/985-increase-nginx-default-security)), and now `trustHosts()`.

**Consent.** Scrolling is not consent, and nothing requires a blocking banner ([EDPB Guidelines 05/2020](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_202005_consent_en.pdf)). PostHog's identifier cookie lasts 365 days ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/docs/libraries/js/persistence)).

**Menu importer.** Modelled on Untappd for Business: match what is certain, leave the rest for a person, publish nothing unreviewed ([Untappd](https://help.untappd.com/hc/en-us/articles/42014018924564-POS-Integration-Toast)).

**Monitoring.** [Healthchecks.io](https://healthchecks.io/pricing/): free for 20 checks; Latvian company, data on Hetzner in Germany ([FAQ](https://healthchecks.io/faq/)).

**English (parked in V3).** 15.2 million foreign tourists in Polish accommodation in 2025 ([GUS](https://stat.gov.pl/dla-mediow/informacje-prasowe/moda-na-polske-2025-rok-pod-znakiem-rekordow-w-turystyce,52,1.html)); 61 million adults recruited into no-alcohol drinks 2022–24 across ten markets ([IWSR](https://www.theiwsr.com/insight/key-statistics-the-no-alcohol-and-low-alcohol-market/)). Machine translation that adds nothing is scaled content abuse ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)); hreflang pairs must be reciprocal ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)).

**Decision records.** All 132 carry an `Executed:` line; this morning 89 done, 11 partly, 6 not done, 12 standing rules, 10 superseded, 2 reversed without a record, 2 not applicable.
